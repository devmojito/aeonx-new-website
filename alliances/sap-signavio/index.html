4 weeks to a process diagnostic.

W1
Connect & extract

W2
Mine & map
Generate the actual process map. Identify the dominant variants, the bottlenecks, the rework loops.
W3
Analyse & benchmark
Quantify the cost of inefficiency. Benchmark against SAP best practice. Identify quick wins.
W4
Recommend
Deliver the diagnostic report with prioritised actions, RISE scoping inputs, and KPI baselines. The first artefact of any transformation.
