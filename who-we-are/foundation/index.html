THE STORY

A short version.

AeonX began in 2020 in Mumbai and Bhuj. The aim was simple: build an SAP partner that ran like a listed company from day one audit-ready, accountable, and on the same balance sheet as the customers it served. We’re an aspiring largest enterprise tech firm in India, built for the Large and MSME segments, the Tier-1 giants can’t serve at our cost, and the boutiques can’t serve at our scale.
Five years in, we ship our own SaaS products on top of the same stack we deliver to clients. AXIOM, our AI platform, closes 70% of L1 SAP tickets in production today.
